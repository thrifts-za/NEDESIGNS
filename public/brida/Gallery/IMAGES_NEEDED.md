## Style rules across all images

- No watermarks, no logos other than Mira jar
- Real-feeling people, no overly white teeth, no influencer poses
- Cream/sage/forest-green palette in props
- Soft natural light. Avoid hard studio rim lights.
- Documentary editorial style, not lifestyle-stock
- Slight grain/film feel is fine
- Prefer 3:2 or 16:10 ratios for photo content; jar product shots can be square
