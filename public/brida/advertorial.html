I'm a 64-year-old retired ICU nurse. I spent 38 years telling patients to "try the cream first before the pills." Then I got Type 2 diabetes in 2021, and by 2023 my own feet were burning so badly at 2am I'd cry into a pillow.

I tried everything my doctor offered. Capsaicin cream — burned worse. Lidocaine patches — fell off in my sleep. Lyrica — worked, sort of, but I was foggy and gained 12 pounds. I asked my endocrinologist about alternatives and she shrugged. "Not much else proven."

So when my daughter sent me a TikTok of a woman crying because her feet stopped burning after applying a green-jar lotion called Mira, I rolled my eyes. Then I bought it. Here's what 30 days looked like.

What Mira actually is

Before I get to the journal, the basics: Mira is a magnesium-chloride and arnica-based topical lotion made by a small company called Brida. It's $79 a jar (and considerably less in their bundle). The active ingredients are magnesium chloride, arnica montana, menthol, ginger root, and wintergreen. No CBD. No prescription.

Magnesium absorbed through the skin is well-documented to calm peripheral nerve excitability. Arnica reduces local inflammation. Menthol is a counter-irritant. None of this is exotic — what's interesting is the combination and concentration.

The 30-day journal

Day 1

Cool, weirdly. Skeptical.

Applied a thick layer to both feet at 9pm. The menthol cooled fast — like icy-hot but lighter. Burning sensation softened within 4–5 minutes. Slept until 3am, then the burning came back. Better than nothing.

Day 7

Slept till 5am. First time since 2023.

Twice-daily application — once after my morning shower, once before bed. Today I slept till 5am without waking. Cried in the kitchen.

Day 14

Walked the dog. A whole mile.

I haven't taken Buddy on a real walk since 2024. Today I walked him to the park and back. My feet didn't burn. They felt like feet.

Day 21

Halved my Lyrica with my doctor's OK.

Saw my endocrinologist. Showed her the journal. She said "let's try cutting the Lyrica in half and see how you feel." A week later — fine. Better, even. The brain fog is lifting.

The honest cons

It's not magic. Three things to know:

1. It works while you use it. Stop applying for 4–5 days and the burning creeps back. This is symptomatic relief, not a cure. (My doctor says no topical is a cure.)

2. The first jar is $79. That's not cheap. The 3-pack at $90 is what makes it actually affordable long-term.

3. The smell. It's herbal — arnica plus menthol plus something earthy. My husband likes it. My grandkids do not. Apply it before they hug you.
