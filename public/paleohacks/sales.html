Why breakfast is where most paleo attempts die

Lunch and dinner are easy on paleo. You eat protein and vegetables. You can hide a paleo dinner inside an Italian restaurant order. You can hide a paleo lunch inside a Chipotle salad bowl.

Breakfast is the trap. Bagels, cereal, toast, pancakes, croissants, muffins. The whole standard American breakfast is grain plus refined sugar plus seed oil. Take all three away and most people don't know what's left.

So they skip breakfast. By 10:30 they're starving. By 11 they're at the vending machine. By noon their blood sugar is wrecked. By 3pm they're crashed and irritable. The diet "doesn't work for them" and they go back to bagels.

The diet you can stick to wins. The diet you can't stick to past Wednesday isn't a diet, it's a vacation.— Emily K., PaleoHacks Recipe Editor
