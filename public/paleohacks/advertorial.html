I Tried Paleo Breakfast For 30 Days as a Tired Mom of 3. Here's the Honest Review.

After three years of cereal, drive-thru muffins, and the 10:30am crash that wrecked every productive morning, I gave a $19.99 paleo breakfast cookbook a chance. Day-by-day journal below.

Sarah J., 38, mom of three Updated May 2026 · 8 min read
Share
My Tuesday morning before vs. after. Same kitchen. Same kids. Different breakfast.

I'm 38 with three kids under 10. By 2024 I was crashing every single morning at 10:30, and I'd convinced myself it was just normal mom life.

The problem was breakfast. I'd been skipping it for years, then grabbing a granola bar around 10am that wasn't holding me. By noon I was irritable, snapping at the kids over snack-pouches, and inhaling whatever leftovers I could find from their plates. By 3pm I was crashed. By 8pm I was finishing a half-bag of pretzels on the couch.

I'd tried paleo before. Twice. Both times I made it to Wednesday morning before giving up because I had no idea what to eat for breakfast that wasn't eggs, eggs, or eggs again.

Then a friend in my mom group sent me a link to a $19.99 paleo breakfast cookbook from a brand called PaleoHacks. She'd lost 11 pounds in six weeks just by changing breakfast. I rolled my eyes. Then I bought it.

What it actually is

Before the journal: this is a digital cookbook from PaleoHacks. PDFs, not a printed book. 60 recipes split into 6 sections: smoothies, egg-free, pancakes/waffles, muffins, skillets, and weekend treats. Every recipe is under 15 minutes. Most are under 10. No grain, no refined sugar, no recipes with 14 obscure ingredients.

The honest cons

1. It's a digital download, not a printed book. If you want a physical book on the counter, you'll spend $4 at FedEx printing the recipes you cook most.

2. It's breakfast-focused. If you want lunch and dinner too, the 3-cookbook bundle is the move. Most readers grab it during checkout.

3. The recipes use almond and coconut flour. If your kid has a tree-nut allergy, about a third of the recipes won't work for you. Coconut-only options are flagged.
