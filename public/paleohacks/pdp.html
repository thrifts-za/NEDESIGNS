Real food, no shortcuts

Every recipe is grain-free, refined-sugar-free, and seed-oil-free by default. Most are dairy-free. We test recipes in real kitchens with real grocery budgets, not test kitchens with $80 bottles of olive oil.

If a recipe needs an obscure ingredient (it rarely does), we name a trader-joe's-tier substitute right next to it. We're not trying to make you spend $40 on monk fruit shipped from Kyoto.

For ingredients we trust most often: pasture-raised eggs, grass-fed butter or coconut oil, almond and coconut flours, wild-caught fish, and seasonal produce. Brand names are noted where it actually matters.

This cookbook is for you if

  • You wake up at 6:45 and need to feed yourself or your family by 7:15.
  • You've tried paleo before but couldn't make it past breakfast without grabbing toast.
  • You're sensitive to gluten, dairy, or refined sugars and "skip breakfast" isn't a real plan.
  • You want recipes your kids will actually eat without negotiation.
  • You've been doing keto and want gentler, broader paleo with more fruit and starch.

Standard breakfast vs. paleo breakfast

Standard breakfast

  • Cereal + skim milk = sugar crash by 10:30
  • Bagel + cream cheese = bloat + brain fog
  • Drive-thru muffin = seed oils + refined flour
  • Skipping = ravenous by lunch, overeats
  • Energy plateaus mid-morning, every day

Paleo breakfast (this book)

  • Real protein + healthy fat = steady fuel till lunch
  • No bloat, no brain fog, no afternoon crash
  • Ingredients you can pronounce, no seed oils
  • Most recipes ready in 12 minutes flat
  • Energy steady all morning into afternoon

Quick answers

How fast do I get the cookbook?

Instant. The download link arrives in your email within 60 seconds of checkout. PDFs work on phone, tablet, laptop, Kindle, anything that opens a PDF.

Is this Whole30 / keto / dairy-free compatible?

About 70% of the recipes are Whole30-compliant out of the box. Keto-friendly options are flagged. Dairy-free swaps are noted on every dairy recipe (almond milk for cream, coconut oil for butter, etc.).

Do I need fancy kitchen equipment?

A blender and a non-stick pan cover 80% of the recipes. The remaining 20% need a sheet pan, a muffin tin, or a waffle iron. No InstantPot, no air fryer, no $400 stand mixer.

Most recipes work as crowd food without needing to mention they're paleo. The bacon-weave sandwich, the churro waffles, the smoothie bowls. Your kids and skeptical spouse won't notice the lack of grain.
